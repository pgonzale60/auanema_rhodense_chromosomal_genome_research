**Figure 2. Chromosome-wide distribution of tandem repeat families, protein-coding genes, and non-coding RNA elements.**

**(A)** Fractional coverage of tandem repeat (TR) families per 100 Kb genomic window displayed across parallel sub-tracks for each chromosome. The upper sub-track (**Total TR**) depicts total genomic TR coverage in dark slate grey, while the lower sub-track (**Families**) shows the stacked fractional composition of specific dominant TR families. The two major TR families retained in somatic X-derived chromosomes (XR-1 and XR-2) are shown in blue and purple, respectively; all other families correspond to TR families found in eliminated regions. Windows overlapping rRNA loci are excluded to avoid conflating tandem ribosomal repeat arrays with the TR families shown. TR families are named according to their genomic location and elimination fate: AT-1 and AT-2 are the conserved terminal repeats present at the ends of all autosomes; AC-I through AC-VI are chromosome-specific central eliminated repeats (one dominant family per autosome); XT-1 and XT-2 are the left and right terminal eliminated repeats of the X chromosome; XC is a minor central eliminated repeat on the X; and XR-1 and XR-2 are the somatic-retained TR families occupying the two somatic domains of the X chromosome.

**(B)** Distribution of protein-coding genes, shown as dark green lines per 100 Kb window.

**(C)** Genome-wide fractional coverage of major RNA element classes (rRNA, tRNA, snRNA, and other ncRNAs) calculated per 10 Kb window and displayed on a log₁₀ scale.

In all panels, GRS (Germline-Restricted Sequence) regions are highlighted with high-contrast soft slate background shading, while somatic-retained regions are displayed on a white background. Internal GRS boundaries are marked by dashed vertical lines with inverted black triangular markers (▼) at the top of each chromosome track. Black rectangles outline the full extent of each chromosome track. Chromosome labels (Chr 1–Chr X) are shown on the right of each facet row.
