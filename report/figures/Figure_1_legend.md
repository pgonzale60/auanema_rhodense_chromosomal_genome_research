# Figure 1 Legend

**Figure 1. Genomic organization and chromatin structure of *Auanema rhodense* reveals evidence of programmed DNA elimination.**

**(A)** Hi-C interaction map of the *A. rhodense* genome assembly showing a checkerboard pattern reflecting cis- and trans-chromosomal interactions across all seven chromosome-level scaffolds (six autosomes and the X chromosome). Dotted lines indicate boundaries of genomic regions separated by telomeric repeat mapping sites. Color scale indicates interaction frequency (log scale).

**(B)** Magnified view of chromosome 5 Hi-C interactions. Dotted lines mark the boundaries between gene-rich segments (GRS) that correspond to telomeric repeat mapping sites and transitions between high and low sequence coverage regions.

**(C)** Repeat density across chromosomes shown as percentage of repetitive DNA per 10 kb window. Each autosome displays three repeat-rich regions (>90% density): one central and two terminal. The X chromosome shows two large terminal repeat-rich regions and two additional regions in the chromosome center. Dotted vertical lines and triangles indicate internal GRS boundaries.

**(D)** Distribution of Nigon elements across chromosomes based on conserved single-copy orthologs (BUSCOs). Protein-coding genes are predominantly localized to sixteen distinct, non-repetitive regions (<10% repeat density per 10 kb window) that show dense Hi-C interactions. Each autosome contains two such regions, while the X chromosome contains four.

**(E)** GC content variation across chromosomes. Central regions display heterogeneous GC content, while distal autosomal regions show relative homogeneity. The X chromosome exhibits distinct patterns with lower GC in the left repeat-rich region and slightly higher GC in the right repeat-rich region compared to autosomal repeat-rich ends.

**(F)** HiFi long-read sequencing coverage across chromosomes. Gene-rich regions display average 236-fold coverage, while repeat-rich regions show approximately 29-fold coverage. The X chromosome gene-rich regions exhibit intermediate coverage (146-fold average), consistent with hemizygosity in males. An additional low-coverage region in the X center is not particularly repeat-rich.

**(G)** Mapping locations of reads containing tandem arrays of the telomeric hexamer repeat TTAGGC (or reverse complement). Telomeric repeat read mapping sites coincide with transitions between gene-rich/repeat-rich regions and high/low coverage boundaries. Each chromosome is partitioned into five segments by these telomeric repeat mapping sites, with the exception of the terminal high-interaction, low-coverage region on the X chromosome right end. Black rectangles outline chromosome boundaries; dotted vertical lines with triangles mark internal segment boundaries.

All panels use "Chr X" labeling convention. Internal GRS boundaries (panels C-G) are shown only where coordinates fall >10 bp from chromosome ends.
