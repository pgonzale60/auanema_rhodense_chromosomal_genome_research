**Figure 3. New telomeres define chromosome break sites in the genome of *Auanema rhodense***
 
**(A)** Long read coverage highlighting the precise position of soft clipped telomeric repeat arrays in most reads aligning on chromosome 5 around position 16,502,174. The part of the sequence matching the motif is highlighted (light blue) as well as the eliminated region.
 
**(B)** The Sequence for Elimination (SFE) associated with telomere addition sites in *A. rhodense* compared to the *O. tipulae* SFE. The SFEs have been aligned to highlight their similarity.
 
**(C)** Single-strand motif match scores across genomic compartments. The horizontal black dotted line marks the minimum score observed at an authentic break site (19.5 bits); four loci in the retained somatic genome exceed this threshold, demonstrating that single-motif affinity alone is insufficient to prevent ectopic somatic fragmentation.
 
**(D)** Dual-strand score evaluation of canonical palindromic pairs with exact 2 bp offset (plus strand score vs. minus strand score). The shaded grey region denotes the dual-strand passing filter defined by a joint score sum ($s_+ + s_- \ge 37.5$ bits, dotted diagonal boundary). All authentic break sites ($n = 28$) fall cleanly within this passing zone (minimum sum = 38.5 bits), completely discriminating them from retained euchromatic pairs ($n = 235$, maximum sum = 36.9 bits) which fail the dual-strand requirement.
