# Figure Legend

**Figure. Chromosome-wide distribution of tandem repeat families, protein-coding genes, and RNA elements**

**A.** Distribution of the dominant tandem repeat (TR) family per GRS region (colored blocks), displayed as fraction coverage per 100 Kb window. TR windows overlapping with RNA-rich regions (>5% combined RNA content in 10 Kb windows) are excluded. Total TR coverage is shown in grey.

**B.** Distribution of protein-coding genes (dark green boxes).

**C.** Genome-wide distribution of major RNA elements (tRNA, rRNA, snRNA, and other RNA) shown as separate line traces. Fractions are calculated per 10 Kb window and displayed on a log₁₀ scale.

GRS regions are highlighted with wheat-colored overlays in all panels. Internal GRS boundaries are indicated by dotted vertical lines with inverted black triangular markers (▼) at the top. Black rectangles outline each chromosome.
