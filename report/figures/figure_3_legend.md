**Figure 3. Programmed DNA elimination (PDE) systematics and chromosome break site architecture in *Auanema rhodense***
 
**(A)** Cladogram of target nematode species based on concatenated 18S and 28S ribosomal DNA sequences, rooted with *Trichinella spiralis* as outgroup. Background shading categorizes taxa by PDE phenotype: Precise PDE (green shading, shared across Rhabditina), Imprecise PDE (amber shading, characteristic of Ascaridomorpha), and No PDE (purple shading, nested within crown *Caenorhabditis* including *C. elegans* and *C. briggsae*). The focal species *Auanema rhodense* is positioned at the top.

**(B)** Long read coverage highlighting the precise position of soft clipped telomeric repeat arrays in most reads aligning on chromosome 5 around position 16,502,174. The part of the sequence matching the motif is highlighted (light blue) as well as the eliminated region.
 
**(C)** The Sequence for Elimination (SFE) associated with telomere addition sites in *A. rhodense* compared to the *O. tipulae* SFE. The SFEs have been aligned to highlight their similarity.
 
**(D)** Single-strand motif match scores across genomic compartments. The horizontal black dotted line marks the minimum score observed at an authentic break site; the score threshold clearly separates authentic chromosome break sites from the retained somatic genome.
